## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "fully remote", "remote-first", "distributed team" *[stated]*
- "reliability", "quality", "attention to detail", "compliance", "security", "documentation" *[DISC: C]*
- "collaborative", "supportive team", "mentoring", "developer experience", "platform team" *[DISC: S]*
- "ownership", "build from scratch", "automate", "eliminate toil", "self-hosted", "cost optimisation" *[inferred from work history]*

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- "hybrid", "office days", "in-office collaboration" (on-site is a deal-breaker; hybrid needs a check with the candidate)
- "pre-sales", "client presentations", "evangelism", "developer advocacy", "networking", sales targets *[DISC: very low I]*. **Matters for Solutions Architect roles:** technical-design SA roles fit better than pre-sales-heavy ones.
- "fast-paced", "constantly shifting priorities", "move fast and break things", "high-pressure" *[DISC: high S + C]* - read the context rather than rejecting outright
- roles described mostly as ticket-driven maintenance or support with little build work *[inferred]*

## Management Style Preferences
*[Typical for an S/CD blend according to DISC - not yet confirmed by the candidate]*
- **Works well:** clear expectations and priorities, advance notice of changes, calm and respectful communication, decisions backed by data, trust to do the job properly.
- **Works less well:** sudden reprioritisation without explanation, confrontational or aggressive management, pressure for instant decisions without the information to make them.

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** lead with careful, reliable delivery backed by numbers (drift detection, SOC-2, P99 latency, 50% cost cut) and with helping teams (internal developer platform, provisioning in under 30 minutes). Keep the tone confident but not salesy - a bold sales pitch will not sound like the candidate.
- **CV:** emphasise reliability, correctness and measurable results; the Goya team-leadership bullet shows the Dominant side.
- **Interviews:** prepare short, confident statements of personal impact, because self-promotion does not come naturally (very low I). Use the Goya leadership and SOC-2 STAR candidates for leadership questions, and the drift-detection and SOC-2 ones for quality and process questions.
- **Don't overstate:** the DISC report is a free short-form estimate - never cite it in applications, and don't claim traits it doesn't support. Never present the candidate as holding a formal lead title.
